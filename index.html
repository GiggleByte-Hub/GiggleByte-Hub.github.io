Chariots of Grace

Ages 7+Bible storiesCharacter & morals

Ride a chariot from Babylon to the throne of heaven. At each of 12 stops, hear a Bible story and make a real-life choice that tests one of the Ten Commandments.

  • Faithful choices earn crowns; wrong ones add burdens
  • Repentance always brings forgiveness — like the prodigal son
  • Angels, manna, moments of grace and a chariot of fire

Rootwords

Ages 7+Geometry & spellingSustainability

Explore real patterns in nature — leaf veins, honeycombs, shells and spider webs — and learn a little math, a little spelling and a lot of care for the planet.

  • Sketch the pattern, then answer geometry questions about it
  • Spell nature words with letter tiles
  • Pick the greener choice and see the real trade-offs

For parents, teachers & Sunday schools

Every GiggleByte game is short enough for one lesson and gentle enough for young players.

  • Each Bible story shows its reference, so you can read the full passage together.
  • Facts in Rootwords come with their sources.
  • No chat, no ads, no accounts — nothing for a child to stumble into.
  • Works on classroom laptops, tablets and phones, even without internet once loaded.
  • Accessible by design: keyboard-friendly, and colour is never the only cue.

About GiggleByte

GiggleByte is a small, independent studio that believes learning should feel like play. We build games that are honest, kind and free — the kind we'd happily hand to our own kids.

Our games are open source, so anyone can see exactly how they work. Have an idea for a new game, or found a bug? We'd love to hear from you.
